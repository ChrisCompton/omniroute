## Description

Rowian Tracker is your own personal fitness coach.

Tell it your age, height, weight, activity level and goal (lose fat, maintain, or build muscle). It works out your daily calories, protein, carbs and fat, a daily step target, and a weekly training plan.

TRACK WHAT YOU EAT
• Search a built-in food list or quick-add any calories and macros
• See calories left and protein, carbs and fat at a glance
• Log by breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks

KNOW WHAT TO DO IN THE GYM
• A weekly split based on how many days you train (2 to 6)
• Sets, reps and rest times matched to your goal and experience
• Form tips for every exercise and a built-in rest timer
• Home version with no-equipment swaps

WALK MORE
• Counts your steps with your iPhone's motion sensor
• A daily step goal and a 7-day history

YOUR DAILY COACH
• A daily summary of what to focus on: protein to go, steps left, today's workout and water
• Weight log with your 2-week trend

Your data stays on your phone. No account, no ads.

Rowian Tracker gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice.
