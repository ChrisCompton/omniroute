# Rowian Tracker: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Rowian Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **What the app stores:** your profile (age, sex, height, weight, goals), food log, weight log, step counts, water intake and workout progress.
- **Where it is stored:** only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party.
- **Motion & Fitness:** with your permission, the app reads your step count from your phone's motion sensor to show your daily steps. The step data stays on your device.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.**
- **Deleting your data:** use "Reset all data" on the Me tab, or delete the app.

Rowian Tracker gives general fitness guidance and is not medical advice.
